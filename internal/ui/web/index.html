Syncs & Backups

Backups keep earlier versions of changed and deleted files so you can go back in time. Syncs copy or mirror folders. Both run on a schedule, at sign-in, or when a particular USB drive is plugged in, and retry on their own.

Time Portal

Browse a backup as it was at any earlier moment and bring back files, including ones that were deleted. Backups made by Enter Space on a Mac, iPhone or iPad open here too.
